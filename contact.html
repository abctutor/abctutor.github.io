Common Questions

Do I need to create an account to book?

No. Just pick a tutor and an open time, fill in your info, and you're booked.

How will I know my booking went through?

You'll see an instant confirmation on screen, plus a confirmation email and text.

Can two families book the same time slot?

No — once a time is booked, it's removed from that tutor's list right away so it can't be booked again.
